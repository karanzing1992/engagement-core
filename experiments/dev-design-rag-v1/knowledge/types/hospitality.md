# Hospitality and Social-Stay UI/UX — Type Graph

Generic type knowledge only; no hotel or client branding, no personal details.
Sources: https://www.w3.org/TR/wcag/ ; https://www.nngroup.com/articles/ten-usability-heuristics/ ; https://designsystem.digital.gov/

## Hospitality information priorities
A visitor first needs location context, accommodation types, important differences, actual photos, clear enquiry or confirmed booking mechanism, directions and operational conditions. Lead with authentic atmosphere and show real accommodation imagery when approved; unrelated destination imagery must be labeled honestly. Show room types and occupancy characteristics without inventing inventory, availability, amenity guarantees, ratings or testimonials.

## Guest journey
Support browsing rooms, comparing dorm/private options, check-in/check-out guidance, access from mobile, native map/directions and unambiguous contact. Distinguish WhatsApp enquiry from instant booking; a confirmed booking requires actual inventory and explicit confirmation. Design for travel conditions including low signal, narrow screens and international language needs.

## Aesthetic direction
Avoid generic hotel template design. Show credible community, local character, quality/cleanliness and a cohesive photographic treatment. Strong hierarchy combines evocative place with practical next actions; do not bury the first enquiry button. Validate that imagery, attribution and captions do not imply another property is the client's property.

## Acceptance
Test navigation/room comparison, inquiry links, form validation, map correctness, keyboard, 320/390/768/1440 responsive checks, WCAG AA and independent artistic critique >=95/100. Final release remains owner-controlled.

# Editorial, Marketing and Content UX — Type Graph

Sources: https://www.nngroup.com/articles/ten-usability-heuristics/ ; https://www.w3.org/TR/wcag/ ; https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Learn_web_development/Core/Accessibility/CSS_and_JavaScript

## Content hierarchy
Establish one compelling lead, clear headings, appropriate content density, source-backed images, readable typography and deliberate progression to desired actions. Captions, link text, accessible alt text and mobile text-safe zones must match actual content.

## Photography and provenance
Track source URL, creator, publication/derivative rights, crop decisions, attribution and factual context; do not pass generic public imagery as an actual business venue or person without verification. Design quality does not authorize publication, sales claims or rights violations.

## Multi-format UX
Account for portrait video, social feeds, landing pages, editorial long reads and mobile preview constraints. Motion, subtitles, color and audio must support meaning without relying on one sense. Keep separation between draft, reviewed and published.

## Acceptance
Review technical render, image rights, captions and independent artistic >=95; owner must approve publishing separately.

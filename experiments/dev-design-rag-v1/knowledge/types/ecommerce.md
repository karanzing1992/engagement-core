# E-commerce and Checkout UI/UX — Type Graph

General references: https://www.w3.org/TR/wcag/ ; https://www.nngroup.com/articles/ten-usability-heuristics/ ; https://baymard.com/research-articles/ecommerce-navigation-best-practice

## Product discovery and trust
Show authentic descriptions, variants, units, costs, stock status and return/shipping terms from verified commerce data. Search, filtering and comparison should reduce decision effort without concealing critical terms. Distinguish exact pricing from estimates.

## Checkout and payment
Make cart and totals transparent. Display taxes, fees, delivery constraints and final confirmation before charge. Preserve entered values and validate accessibly. Avoid dark patterns, misleading urgency and fabricated social proof. Never place orders or charge payment instruments based only on artistic review authorization.

## Accessibility and performance
Fast mobile pages, labeled forms, error recovery, mobile focus order, readable price contrast and no unverified claims are necessary. Validate state transitions with synthetic purchases, redacted receipts, no real payment during QA unless separately authorized.

## Acceptance
A designer may pass artistic 95 while checkout is broken; technical and explicit owner financial release gates remain independent.

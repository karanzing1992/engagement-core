## 2026 trend interpretation — not mandatory design rules
Expressive editorial typography; context-sensitive photography; tactile but restrained visual identity; modular design tokens; less interchangeable AI-generated card layouts; trustworthy AI-mediated UX; meaningful feedback; storytelling with usable navigation; intentional mobile layouts; purposeful, optional motion. These observations are synthesized from the cited awards and NN/g's 2026 discussion of differentiation through trust, research and judgment, not a universal ranking of styles.
NN/g State of UX 2026 (2026-01-16): https://www.nngroup.com/articles/state-of-ux-2026/

## Apply award inspiration safely
For every proposed award-derived pattern: name user outcome, source, whether the asset and concept are licensed, expected performance impact, accessible fallback, mobile behavior and original execution decision. Reject a visually impressive treatment that breaks basic tasks, use of authentic assets, WCAG or owner brand instructions. Recognition does not grant reproduction rights.

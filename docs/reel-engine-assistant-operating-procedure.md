# Reel Engine — assistant operating procedure (2026-10-11)

## Mandatory fresh-creative and originality gate — 12 October 2026

**Applies to every new Moksha daily knowledge Story/Reel and recurring original social creative.** Reuse our first-party FFmpeg/Reel Engine, templates, typography, captions, render/export logic and the *real, verified official Moksha logo*. **Never silently reuse yesterday's creative.**

1. **Fresh video per episode.** Search unused original Moksha footage first; next use new verified-licensed footage actually relevant to the scene, from **anywhere in India, with Goa preferred**. Do not use unrelated foreign locations. Do not recycle footage, exact shots, completed reels, scene sequences or video backgrounds from prior published episodes unless the user explicitly requests a reprise and it is labeled in the brief.
2. **Fresh, appropriate sound.** Select a different, licensing-verified music bed or deliberate new natural field recording for each episode; mix ambience to support the scenes. A previous completed video's soundtrack is never a generic emergency audio-fix source. A silent intermediate track is a failed render, not grounds to copy old music.
3. **Provenance manifest before render.** Record episode identifier/topic, each footage source URL or original asset ID, source/creator, license and attribution requirements, evidence for India/Goa geography where documented, file SHA-256 (and perceptual similarity signal where available), trim/timecode, previous-use history, and fresh audio source/license/ID. Unverified geography or licensing must be explicitly marked *unverified*; do not claim publishing clearance.
4. **Originality gate before approval.** Compare candidate assets against prior episode manifests, previously published asset identifiers and available hashes/visual/audio similarity. Reject knowingly duplicated visuals, music, creative sequence, off-theme scenery, silent audio, unsupported factual claims or overlapping caption layers. Rotate morning topics, story structure, scenes and mood. Use actual subject-relevant visuals (walking when instructing walking; sitting when instructing sitting), not generic filler.
5. **No concealed fallback.** If stock access, license verification, original footage materialization or sound sourcing fails, try other authorized sources. If still blocked, report the actual blocker and produce a *clearly labeled incomplete draft* only when useful. Do not label recycled footage/music as a fresh final or publish-ready master. User may expressly approve a reuse exception.
6. **Quality and authorization.** Deliver a private 1080×1920, 30 fps master and a smaller review copy if useful, inspect actual text framing, audio continuity, fade, logo fidelity and scene cuts. Content production alone does not authorize publishing or scheduling. Do not publish without explicit approval and verified rights.
7. **Implementation truthfulness.** This section is a mandatory *editorial/operating rule*, not proof of a deployed automated duplicate detector, production asset database, new rights clearance system or new runtime release. Until enforcement tooling is independently tested, the editor must review manifests and prior-use evidence manually; state any limitations.

For the morning informational series: no quiz, question prompt, CTA or product pitch. Keep the official logo subtle; do not write the brand name in creative/caption unless explicitly directed otherwise. Maintain informative, nature-centered and science-grounded content.

## Visual consistency, approved transparent logo and caption-timing standard — 12 October 2026

**Mandatory for new Moksha videos.** Choose ONE creative representation per video: **fully illustrated/animated** throughout, OR **live-action with real people** throughout. Do not cut between illustrated characters/graphic-only scenes and footage of real people within the same episode without the operator's explicit exception. Animated informational motion-graphics may depict a sun, light cues, leaves, and a body clock; they count as the fully animated route. The existing fresh-creative rule still applies. Use fresh licensed/open-source video and audio when suitable, but reuse neither old footage nor old sound by default.

**Logo, exact identity and transparency:** use *only* the approved, authentic Moksha official logo asset (Drive ID `1XwmlYRfjfkcnbEOlJLNJc8bGHm9uxkyv`, SHA-256 `76e89b83667827da4b5d89143fb267f4000a9392eff685129c838c3d0b24748c`). Video overlays must use a **true-alpha transparent variant**, not the original gold-on-black JPEG rectangle. Prefer a verified official transparent PNG if available; otherwise derive alpha by removing the black background from that exact authenticated gold-on-black official source, **without redrawing/altering the artwork**. Verify the resulting PNG contains meaningful transparent pixels, clean edges, no solid black rectangle and intact golden mark/wordmark. Preserve the source hash in the manifest; derived PNG's hash must be recorded separately. Keep it subtle, without the brand name elsewhere.

**Typography and timing:** use a single on-screen caption/title system, not multiple conflicting subtitle layers. Strong legibility: 1080x1920 safe frame; horizontal margins >= 100 px; avoid top/bottom UI overlays; select high-contrast, restrained typography; 1-2 short readable lines at a time; match text to the precise on-screen idea, with no line lingering into an unrelated scene. Provide a comfortable dwell (roughly >= 1.2s per phrase, scaled for reading complexity), and entry/exit transitions. Ensure visible text is not clipped/overlapping the logo or social UI safe area. Review frames near cuts and mid-cues, including at mobile downscale.

**Recommended existing free/open-source timing paths (research basis 2026-10-12):**
- **FFmpeg libass / ASS subtitle animation**: immediate first-party-compatible solution for frame-accurate, timecoded phrases, alignment and styling. Verify `ffmpeg -filters` lists `ass`/libass; use ASS timecodes at 0.01s resolution and source-controlled script. Docs: https://github.com/FFmpeg/FFmpeg/blob/master/libavfilter/vf_subtitles.c ; https://github.com/zeke/nice-ass-captions (examples of ASS styled caption effects).
- **Remotion + @remotion/captions**: candidate for more advanced per-word highlighting and page switches with a declared frame timeline; NOT deployed, and NOT a dependency for today's FFmpeg render. Docs: https://www.remotion.dev/docs/captions/displaying and https://www.remotion.dev/docs/timing .
- For narrated clips, base word-level timing on actual narration alignment; for text-only informational guides, use deliberate authored phrase/scene cues, not Whisper-generated or simulated speech timestamps.

**QC evidence per episode:** record the selected visual mode (`animation_only` or `real_people_live_action`), original logo SHA, derived PNG alpha inspection, caption script path, minimum cue dwell and safe-area check, scene text screenshots, audio source and license, asset originality, technical ffprobe, and publishing-rights state. If source rights, logo authenticity, or full-style consistency cannot be demonstrated, retain PRIVATE REVIEW status and disclose gaps. No publishing/scheduling without explicit authorization. This is editorial/source-operating guidance only; automatically enforced visual-mode classification or automated caption-QC is not implied by the document.

## Default: finish the user's video before extending infrastructure

When the user attaches videos in ChatGPT and asks for a final cut, **use the attached original bytes directly in the available container runtime**, rather than redirecting the user to Canva, Descript, GitHub Actions or Drive. The 231 MB source of 11 October was already automatically mountable in `/mnt/data`; the failure was caused by choosing a Python runner that returned `ClientError` and then trying connector egress that returned `BLOCKED_FILE_REFERENCE`.

1. Call container-backed runtime to inspect `/mnt/data`, then `ffprobe` the exact original file paths. If an attachment is not automatically mounted, use `files.list` to locate its exact `file_id`, then `files.materialize` it. Never invent or infer a filename/path from a file card.
2. Normalize mixed-video frame rates and pixel aspect ratios before concatenating. The FFmpeg concat filter requires `setsar=1` for every input; the two real uploaded vertical clips had different FPS and source audio availability. Preserve source audio and use silent padding or a user-approved source bed only when needed.
3. Keep footage Goa/India-relevant; do not substitute unrelated foreign stock. Respect the verified original logo SHA-256 `76e89b83667827da4b5d89143fb267f4000a9392eff685129c838c3d0b24748c` before using the Drive-origin original. Do not fabricate a logo.
4. Render a **private** finished MP4 immediately. Verify duration, audio stream, 9:16 aspect ratio, actual on-screen text readability and logo. Return only sandbox paths verified as existing by the container, together with optional smaller social-sharing export.
5. Publishing/scheduling requires a separate explicit user request; generating a finished Reel is not authorization to post.
6. When the container environment cannot process the attachment, use the **first-party WordPress admin Tools → Reel Uploads** (currently hosted in `src/class-grec-media-ingest.php`) and its 4 MiB resumable chunk uploader. Private video input and rendered output stay outside public webroot and the GitHub OIDC worker checks the queue every 30 minutes. Do not tell the user to split or re-upload manually without first trying the native file mount.
7. The new Reel Engine 0.5 endpoints in `upload_api.py`/`multi_api.py` are another authenticated 8 MiB streaming route for **a separately deployed persistent engine**. Their GHCR container image is not proof of a public server or GPT-to-worker egress. Use a private HTTPS worker configured with a secure `REEL_ENGINE_TOKEN` before offering them as live endpoints.
8. Capture workflow errors precisely: local runtime `ClientError`, connector `BLOCKED_FILE_REFERENCE`, WordPress deployment HTTP 401, geometry mismatch (SAR), transcoding failure, or actual engine error are different failure classes. Never call one the other or claim the final exists until `ffprobe` verifies the exact output.

08 / COMMON QUESTIONS

Before you visit
Moksha in Goa.

Useful answers for first-time guests. For individual health concerns about heat or cold exposure, seek professional medical advice.

Where can I find a wood-fired sauna in North Goa?

Moksha Wellness is on Temple Road in Arambol, North Goa. Our sauna uses wood-fired heat in a ground-floor jungle sanctuary. Message us ahead so we can prepare it.

Can I book sauna and ice bath together?

Yes. Fire & Ice Reset combines a wood-fired sauna with guided cold immersion and recovery time. The current menu lists the experience at ₹1,200.

What is included with a mineral mud or aloe bath?

Our proposed V2 bath format pairs a freshly prepared bath ritual with a sauna session. Tell us which bath you prefer, and we will confirm inclusions and price before you book.

Is Moksha near Arambol Beach?

We are on Temple Road in Arambol, Goa. Use the directions link for the current route rather than relying on an estimated walking time.

Can I join yoga or a community session?

Free yoga or meditation is usually at 5 PM, followed by a community ritual at 6 PM. Ask our team to confirm that day's schedule.

Can I book a private visit?

Yes. Private sessions are available during the day. Message the team with your preferred time and group size for options.
